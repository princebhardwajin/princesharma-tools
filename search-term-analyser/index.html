How to download your Amazon search term report
  1. In the Amazon Ads console, open your Sponsored Products campaigns.
  2. Open the Search terms tab (or go to Reports → Create report → Sponsored Products → Search term).
  3. Pick a date range of at least 30 days.
  4. Download as .csv or .xlsx and drop it above.
  1. Log in to Flipkart Ads and open Reports.
  2. Choose the Search term (query) report for your PLA campaigns.
  3. Pick a date range of at least 30 days and download the .csv.
  4. Drop the file above. The start and end date lines at the top are handled for you.
